## What is Pagewright?

Pagewright is a **file-based CMS** that lets you build and manage a website by writing instructions, not configuring databases or installing plugins.

You tell Pagewright what you want to change. Pagewright compiles your content into HTML. Your site runs anywhere that supports PHP - even the cheapest shared hosting.

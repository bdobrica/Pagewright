What is Pagewright?

Pagewright is a file-based CMS that lets you build and manage a website by writing instructions, not configuring databases or installing plugins.

You tell Pagewright what you want to change. Pagewright compiles your content into HTML. Your site runs anywhere that supports PHP - even the cheapest shared hosting.

Getting Started

Key Features

No Database

Everything is stored in files. Your content is portable, version-controllable, and easy to backup.

LLM-Powered

Describe changes in natural language. Pagewright validates and applies them safely.

Theme System

Themes define layout, components, and styling. Switch themes without losing content.
